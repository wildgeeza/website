01 / About

The bridge between
art and code.

Technical Art. UI Systems. Ambitious Visuals.

I’m a Senior Technical Artist with 25+ years of experience working between art, design and engineering, specialising in real-time workflows, tools, optimisation and visual systems for AAA games and commercial projects.

I’m driven by the same challenge wherever it appears: take a complex visual idea, solve the technical problem behind it and turn it into something artists can build and players can experience.

I learn by doing. I solve by building. I’m always looking for the next technical problem to solve.
