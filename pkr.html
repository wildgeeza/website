< Lead Flash UI Developer - PKR

PKR was an online poker room that allowed users to play poker in a 3D reality setting. The site customized 3D graphics and player character animation during poker play. The characters incorporated body language, expressions, chip tricks and poker tells to make the game more realistic. PKR catered for all online 3D gambling experiences including, Poker, Blackjack and Roulette.

Core responsibilities included the introduction of new, maintenance & improvement of all existing Flash/Scaleform GFx based user interface experiences. This includes all menus, statistical widgets and controls within the game and lobby experiences for PC/Mac desktops, along with the iOS and Android mobile environments.

Tech: Flash, Actionscript, Scaleform and Photoshop
