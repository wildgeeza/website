01 / Overview

iOS Companion based app

Vibe coded SwiftUI iOS deployment iPhone & Apple Watch Adobe Photoshop

GolfTracker is an iPhone and Apple Watch golf round-tracking app.

You start a round on the phone with a course name and number of holes, then use the Watch to begin each hole, choose par and club, and record shots.

The phone tracks GPS positions, draws each shot on a satellite/hybrid map, calculates yardage, and keeps a hole-by-hole scorecard.

02 / The challenge

Simplified golf shot tracking.

Create an easy to use, companion based app, that allows for quick recording of golf shot location and yardage for future club selection reference.

The technical challenge was to learn the full stack for creation of the app. From Vibe SwiftUI coding, to deployment to device through XCode.

Design requirements
  • Track the distance of each golf shot based on a selected club.
  • Support manual and automatic (via swing and audio) shot recording.
  • Record the GPS location of each shot for future reference.
  • Scalable for different number of golfing rounds.
